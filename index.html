How it works

Three things,
done properly.

01

Import your CSV — any provider

Drop in a Catapult OpenField, STATSports CoachMePlus/Sonra, or PlayerTek export. Loadmark handles the format differences, date quirks, and column aliases automatically. No remapping required.

catapult · statsports · playertek
02

Sessions scored against your match data

Volume and intensity scores are computed automatically using your team's own game data as the reference — benchmarked to 65-minute match eligibility thresholds, not arbitrary defaults. HSD recalculates if you change speed thresholds, retroactively.

volume score · intensity score · load AU
03

Training stress balance, always current

Acute and chronic EWMA for all six volume metrics, per player, updated on every import. Colour-coded status flags — optimal, caution, high-risk — built from your full session history, not just the last 28 days.

acute · chronic · TSB ratio
